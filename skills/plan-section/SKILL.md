# Plan a manuscript section

Read `.research-assistant.json` and the configured project description. Find the approved nodes relevant to the requested section and read their full files. Read relevant freeform directions and pending proof plans as possible approaches, keeping them distinct from established results. Treat failed plans as recorded obstructions.

Propose a section outline with subsection goals, result order, and the exact node IDs each part uses. Identify dependencies and gaps, including intermediate results that still need proof. Do not present draft nodes or speculative ideas as proved facts. If there are too few approved results to support the requested argument, say what is missing.
